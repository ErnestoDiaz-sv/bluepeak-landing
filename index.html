How it works

Three steps from the service call to a clear answer.

Start a case

Enter the equipment brand, model, and what you're observing — high suction, low delta-T, locked compressor, whatever the complaint is.

Upload the service manual

Drop in the OEM PDF. BluePeak indexes it and uses it to back every finding with a manual citation. Skip this step if you don't have it.

Get ranked hypotheses + tests

BluePeak returns a prioritized list of root causes, the evidence level for each, and the exact field tests to confirm or rule them out.

Resolve and document

Mark the case resolved. The full analysis is saved to your case history for review, handoff, or callbacks.
