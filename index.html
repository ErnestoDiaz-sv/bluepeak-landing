FAQ

What happens to our data?

Your cases and manuals are stored encrypted and kept separate from other shops. We don't sell your data or use it for ads. Full details on our Security page.

How accurate is it?

It passed 11 of 12 test cases built from real OEM manuals in our automated checks. It's still an AI, so it can be wrong. That's why every answer shows the manual page it came from and a confidence label, and why it tells your tech what to test before ordering parts.

Does it replace my techs' judgment?

No. It gives them a faster starting point. The tech on site makes the call.

What doesn't it do?

It's not for emergencies. Gas leaks, carbon monoxide, and live electrical hazards follow your safety protocol first. It works best with clear, text-based manuals; poorly scanned PDFs can index badly.

Which equipment does it cover?

Residential split systems, heat pumps, and variable-speed equipment are the focus today.

Can it connect to our software?

There's an API and an MCP server today. Tell us what you use in the form and we'll talk.
